Oracle Enochian (assets/fonts/OracleEnochian.ttf)
=================================================

A font for The Foul-Mouthed Oracle's "Enochian script" switch: John Dee's 21 Enochian letters (Sloane MS 3188,
f. 104, 6 May 1583) placed on the Latin letters A-Z and a-z.

Glyph drawings
--------------
"Enochian - Un.svg", "Enochian - Pa.svg", ... "Enochian - Ceph.svg" (22 files, Wikimedia Commons,
Category:Enochian letters), by Лорд Алекс (Lord Alex), own work, uploaded 28 March 2018.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Enochian_letters
Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Changes made
------------
The outlines were converted to TrueType curves, scaled, set on a common baseline with side spacing, and mapped to
the Latin letters (C and K: Veh; I and J: Gon; U and V: Van; W: two Vans side by side; Y: Gon with a point).
Font assembled in 2026 by Gerad Smith.
